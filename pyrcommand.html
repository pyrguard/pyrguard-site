Command software

PyrCommand Alpha

PyrCommand is actively developed wildfire incident-command software for geographic planning, incident management, resource coordination, reconnaissance, and containment workflows.

Current Alpha Capabilities

Implemented alpha workflows

Geographic incident planning

Explore incident geography and use map layers to organize planning context.

Incident management

Use incident interfaces to organize incident context and manage response workflows.

Resource coordination and reconnaissance

Coordinate resources and use reconnaissance workflows to incorporate observations into planning.

Containment objectives

Set and track containment objectives and work through related actions in the alpha interface.

Experimental or developing

Incident simulation

Explore planning and coordination through experimental scenarios and simulated incident data.

Operations log and after-action analysis

Developing interfaces for reviewing incident activity, objectives, and simulated outcomes.

Tactical telemetry

Experimental displays for incident status, asset status, and simulated signals.

Foundational concepts

Mobile observer mode

A concept for viewing incident context and updates on mobile devices.

Cloud incident sessions

Early work on preserving and sharing incident context across sessions.

Development status. PyrCommand is alpha software for development and evaluation. Operational deployment, certified emergency response, and validated wildfire-spread prediction are outside the scope of this alpha.
